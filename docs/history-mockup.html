What I understood

  • Match row: top line has date and time, number of rounds and the match status (Completed, or Forfeit). Below it, your name on the left, the opponent on the right and the score in the middle, green for a win and red for a loss.
  • Click a match: a window opens with the same header and one compact row per round. Each round row shows the target and numbers, and each player's time (or Timeout), points and a Winner label. No steps yet.
  • Click a round: a second, smaller window opens on top. It shows the target and numbers, then you on the left and the opponent on the right, each with time, points, result and the steps they took.
  • Closing: X, Esc or a click outside closes only the top window. On a phone both are full screen.
